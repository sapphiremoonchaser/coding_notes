# Regression Coefficient ($\beta$)

The regression coefficient measures the magnitude and direction of the relationship between a predictor and an outcome.

## In OLS regression
The coeffcient represents the expected change in the dependent variable for a one-unit increase in the predictor, holding other variable constant.

If:
    $\beta$ > 0, the relationship is positive

If: 
    $\beta$ < 0, the relationship is negative

  
## In Log Models
The coefficient represents the change in the outcome for a percentage change in the predictor.


## In Poisson Regression
The coefficient represents the change in the log of expected outcomes.

Exponentiating the coefficient gives an incidence rate ratio (IRR), which tells you the multiplicative effect on expected counts.
